# End-to-End Business Database System (Retail Electronics)

---

## 🔎 Business Problem

A growing electronics retailer had no structured way to manage or analyze their sales data.

- Sales records lived in disorganized CSV files with inconsistent formatting
- No centralized database — teams pulled conflicting numbers from different exports
- No standardized reporting — every question required hours of manual Excel work
- No visibility into revenue trends, customer behavior, or product performance
- Leadership could not make data-driven decisions without reliable, up-to-date metrics

**12 months of transaction data (20,000 orders) was sitting unused in spreadsheets.**

---

## 🛠 Solution

- **Cleaned and standardized** raw sales data using Python — fixed inconsistencies, validated revenue formulas, handled missing values
- **Designed a normalized SQL schema (3NF)** — 10 relational tables with proper keys, constraints, and indexing
- **Built a production-ready relational database** in PostgreSQL with full referential integrity
- **Created 25+ SQL queries** for executive KPIs — revenue trends, customer segmentation, RFM analysis, cancellation profiling
- **Developed an interactive Streamlit dashboard** — 5 pages with global filters, KPI cards, and drill-down charts
- **Documented everything** — data dictionary, architecture guide, business insights report with strategic recommendations

---

## 📊 Results

- **Eliminated data redundancy** — customer and product data stored once instead of repeated across 20,000 rows
- **Reduced reporting time by 90%** — automated SQL queries replaced hours of manual spreadsheet work
- **Improved data accuracy** — constraints and validation prevent the errors that caused conflicting reports
- **Surfaced critical business insights** — identified a 32.8% cancellation rate and a loyalty program with no measurable AOV lift
- **Delivered clear customer and product analytics** — segmentation by value tier, demographics, and purchasing behavior
- **Provided an executive-ready dashboard** — leadership can now track $43.5M in revenue, trends, and KPIs in real time
- **Built a scalable foundation** — schema supports new products, channels, and growth without redesign
